about me

I hold a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF), where I was formerly an Early Stage Researcher in the European project Disc4All MSCA-2020-ITN-ETN GA: 955735. I also hold a degree in Mechanical Engineering (2017) and a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, with a specialization in Biomechanics (2020), from the University of Santiago de Chile (USACH). My research focuses on patient-personalized finite element modelling, multiscale mechano-transport simulations, and data-driven stratification of intervertebral disc degeneration.
